= The Fleet That Had to Refuse

== The customer story

Northstar Claims has completed Sovereign AI 401 for one confidential
claims-assistant workload. The next business request is to expand that service
across three sites without turning one trusted node into an unsupported fleet
claim. A policy rollout, stale quote, revoked node, verifier outage, or capacity
spike must change the fleet decision immediately and visibly.

In this 75–90 minute Sovereign AI 501 lab, you act as the qualification lead.
You will run the same fleet contract through baseline, rollout, freshness,
revocation, dependency, admission, correlation, and human-review conditions.

== Customer outcome

You leave with a bounded fleet qualification package that explains:

* which three node records were evaluated under one correlation identity;
* why unsafe or inconsistent evidence produced `REFUSE_PROMOTION`;
* why 12 admitted and 6 refused operations are a rehearsal fixture, not capacity data;
* why zero confidential guests, TDX quotes, protected resources, and model executions
  prevents a live confidential-AI claim; and
* which participant artifacts were removed versus which Launchpad-owned resources
  remain until platform reclaim.

All supplied evidence is **REHEARSAL**. This lab does not observe a confidential
guest, current Intel TDX quote, Trustee verifier/KBS, protected-resource release,
or model execution. The exit artifact requests human review; it grants no
catalog, certification, ordering, promotion, or publication authority.
